Community standards

Server rules

These are the rules that players are expected to follow on the server.

  • Be respectful to other players and staff.
  • No cheating, hacking, or exploiting bugs.
  • Do not grief or destroy other players' builds.
  • Follow staff instructions at all times.
  • No racism. ESPECIALLY NO NWORD!!!!!!
  • No swears of any kind.
  • No advertising other servers or websites.
  • Do not share personal information of yourself or others.
  • Keep chat appropriate for all ages.
  • Do not spam or flood the chat.
  • Do not impersonate staff or other players.
  • Do not use offensive or inappropriate language.
  • No doxxing or DDOSing at any point.
  • Staff should NOT abuse.
  • FAILURE TO COMPLY WITH THESE RULES WILL RESULT IN PUNISHMENT!
